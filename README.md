# Afri Delicacy

A location-aware marketplace for discovering, ordering, and visiting kitchens, restaurants, and hotels serving authentic African local delicacies.

## Vision

Afri Delicacy connects customers with local food providers. A customer can discover nearby kitchens, restaurants, or hotels, browse available meals, contact a chef, place an order for delivery or pickup, or choose to visit the food provider directly.

## Core users

- **Customer:** discovers food providers, browses meals, orders food, manages addresses and contact details, and communicates with chefs.
- **Food Provider / Chef:** manages a kitchen, restaurant, or hotel profile, food catalogue, availability, orders, and customer communication.
- **Super Administrator:** manages the whole platform, users, providers, food listings, orders, reports, moderation, and platform settings.

## Planned MVP

1. Customer registration and login
2. Food-provider registration and profile
3. Nearby provider discovery
4. Search and food categories
5. Food listing with photos, descriptions, prices, and availability
6. Provider profile with address, phone, WhatsApp, opening hours, and location
7. Shopping cart and order placement
8. Delivery or pickup/visit option
9. Customer dashboard and order history
10. Chef/provider dashboard
11. Super-admin dashboard
12. Basic customer-provider messaging

## Planned advanced features

- GPS/map-based discovery and directions
- WhatsApp integration
- AI food discovery and ordering assistant
- AI-assisted customer/chef conversations
- Ratings and reviews
- Notifications
- Online payments
- Delivery management
- Multi-country African cuisine support
- Mobile application
- Analytics and reporting

## Development approach

The project will be built incrementally. We will establish the specification and architecture first, then implement the MVP feature by feature, test it, and deploy it before adding advanced functionality.
